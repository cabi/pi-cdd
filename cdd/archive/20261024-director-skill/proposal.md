# director-skill

## Why

The loop has a director seat — the session that dispatches first-class runners, gates their milestones, keeps the only memory (the PF ledger), and blesses the merge — but nothing in the corpus teaches it. The director law lives in volatile session memory and tribal recall of the archived runs: every director re-derives the protocol from scratch, and the pi/intercom delivery mechanics (idle panes, crashed runner exits, reviewer leaves) are known only to whoever last hit them. The bundled kit has the review procedures (three review skills) and the triage law (cdd-backlog-triage); the director seat is the missing face.

## What Changes

- `skills/cdd-director/SKILL.md` — a fifth bundled skill carrying the director law. **The law (7 points)**: (1) you are the verifier, never the author — every green claim arrives with command + tail, every merge gets an independent verification run; (2) milestones are the only control points — four asks per run (gate+proposal+R1 / draft-green+R2 / implementation green / Close+hold), silence between them; (3) let the doors teach — never pre-narrow what they check, a door firing mid-run is the system working; (4) lawful shape before bypass — no silent `--no-verify`, a stop-before-execution condition usually exposes a lawful amend shape, else manual door runs + at-commit green + a ledger entry; (5) the ledger is the only memory — session memory is stale by default, revalidate before dispatch, harvest findings in flight; (6) spawn briefs carry the protocol — family rule, two axes = two cold sessions = two findings files, briefs under the change's reviews/, "you are a leaf: no push, no merge, no orchestrating"; (7) escalate to the human — unfencing, destructive operations, anything the law is silent on, model-pair overrides, bypass authorization. **The milestone gate table** — what the director checks at each of the four asks (M1 gate honesty and sponsorship shape; M2 disposition quality and scope-shrink honesty; M3 evidence tails and RED-first recorded; M4 independent verification, bypass narrative, ff-only blessing, director pushes). **The evidence protocol** — green claims cite exact command + tail excerpt; the director re-runs gates itself before blessing; merges are ff-only on held branches; only the director pushes. **Findings harvest** — mint PFs in flight through the backlog op, revalidate the ledger before every dispatch, three-strikes forces dispositions, watch items stay watch. **Delivery mechanics (pi-specific, first-class)** — replies to idled panes fail ("No pending ask"), follow with a send; crashed runner panes post-delivery are benign exits, verify the work landed; reviewer leaves report to the director — relay to the runner; re-list the roster before re-addressing; nudges after 10+ minutes idle are the runner checking on you — answer them. **Anti-patterns** — pre-narrowing door checks, trusting green claims without tails, dispatching from session memory, direct edits on contract files, two writers on one repo, letting a runner merge or push. The skill cites the loop law it operationalizes (rules 2a/2b, 9a, 11, 13) without restating it, and cites no run history — self-contained prose, no session memory.
- `skills/cdd-director/assets/mission-brief.md` — the instantiable mission-brief template (the director's equivalent of the reviewer briefs): mission as ONE bounded outcome; out-of-scope boundary (what is explicitly out); context files to read first; the protocol skeleton (gate → R1-if-T1/T2 → draft → R2 → RED-first acceptance → implementation → R3 two axes → strict+guard → feature+archive pair-commit); reviewer constraints (family rule, floor model, free rungs opportunistic, floor-down = stop); milestone asks with WAIT; first action = verify the premise on disk THEN a handshake ask; branch name; never push.
- Discovery rides the existing `pi.skills` registration (`"./skills"` in package.json) — no manifest change.
- `tests/smoke.mjs` gains S38, a new check following S33/S37's structural pattern (frontmatter name matches directory, bounded description, when/whenever routing clause) plus the director-law anchors, the milestone gate table, the evidence protocol, the delivery mechanics, and the mission-brief asset. S33 and S36 stay untouched — their contracts are the review skills' findings grammar and the five reviewer briefs; a director skill carries neither.
- The gherkin living spec (owner of the skills/ + tests/smoke.mjs surface) gains exactly 1 ADDED requirement pinning the bundled director skill's contract, its scenarios pinned to the smoke suite.

## Solutions Considered

- gherkin (capability): owns the bundled-skills surface — the Code lines of "Reviews run cold", "Reviewer briefs live next to their skills", and "The backlog triage skill is bundled" already claim `skills/*/SKILL.md` and `tests/smoke.mjs`; adopted — one ADDED requirement there instead of minting a new capability that would split skills ownership.
- The archived runs under `cdd/archive/`: the primary sources the law is distilled from — the bypass withdrawn for a lawful amend shape, the false green that passed a self-check and a milestone gate until a cold reviewer ran the suite, the per-axis findings split, the mission once dispatched for work already done. Adopted as evidence base only: the skill teaches the distilled law as self-contained prose and cites no run, change, or PF id (the ADR-0012 spirit — a hand-maintained index of history rots; the archive greps forward).
- The four bundled skills: adopted as the kit the director seat completes — review procedures (cdd-spec-review, cdd-code-review, cdd-adr-review) and triage law (cdd-backlog-triage); the director skill names when they fire (the milestone gate table) and never restates their procedure.
- Extending S33 vs a new S38: S33's assertions are review-skill-specific (severity/disposition/blocker findings grammar); adopted S38 so each pin keeps a single-minded contract (the S37-vs-S33 split is the precedent).
- README/docs page instead of a skill: rejected — skills are the surface pi discovers and routes by frontmatter; a docs page does not route and duplicates the skill's audience.

## Non-Goals

- No validator, guard, or law-text change: the director law advises, it never enforces — gate authority stays with the deterministic doors and the human (rule 2a's own frame).
- No change to the review skills' content: the director skill fires them at milestones; their charters stay theirs.
- No runner-side skill: the mission brief is a template the director instantiates; runners stay briefed from paths on disk (rule 2b's frame).
- No automation of milestone asks or intercom mechanics: the skill describes the director's moves for a human or agent in the seat; nothing is scripted.
- No ADR: bundling a docs skill is reversible, unsurprising, and crosses no capability trade-off.
