
# loop-law-hardening

## Why

Four blind spots the completed runs actually hit: a same-family reviewer is a structural echo chamber (shared lineage biases review what the author cannot see — nothing in law or guidance prevents it today); R3's two axes in one session leak the first axis's framing into the second; ticks are treated as history (a Close-phase finding that invalidates a ticked artifact leaves a stale tick — no un-tick/re-verify duty, no blocker when a ticked artifact is silently re-edited); and pins that copy a fragment of the door instead of invoking it stay green through real regressions — in the 20261021-runner-ergonomics run, fragment-style pins stayed green through a module-scope ReferenceError that only the real-door pin caught (session account; the pins are on disk: tests/acceptance/gherkin-guard-placeholder.test.js H1/H2 vs tests/smoke.mjs S15). Meanwhile reviewer briefs are improvised at every spawn: charters live in the skills, but the instantiation — change identity, pinned read state, session requirements — is reinvented per review.

## What Changes

- **Rule 2b gains the author-family clause** (rendered from `lib/loop-law.mjs`): the cold reviewer's model family MUST differ from the author's (family = same provider or model lineage, relative wording — no vendor names in law); when no other-family reviewer can run, the loop stops and asks the human sponsor — never silently degrade — and any lawful fallback records its form and justification per the existing recording duty.
- **Rule 2b gains two-axes-two-sessions; rule 2a carries a cross-reference**: R3's standards and spec axes run as two separate cold sessions, each with its own brief — one session doing both axes leaks the first axis's framing into the second. R3 keeps one findings file (`reviews/r3-code-review.md`), each axis writing its own section.
- **New rule 11a "Ticks are claims, not history"** (literal in `lib/templates.mjs` `RULES_MD`): a Close-phase review finding or a door refusal that invalidates a ticked artifact voids the tick — un-tick, edit, re-run the item's verify-by, record the re-tick inline (`re-verified after <review point>`); an unrecorded re-edit of a ticked artifact is a blocker at archive (sub-lettered under rule 11 because the duty fires at Close — the voiding triggers are Close-phase findings and door refusals, and the blocker lands at archive itself; rule 6's tasks scaffold already carries tick discipline).
- **Rule 7 gains the pin-realism sentence** (literal): a pin for door behavior invokes the door's real entry point — a real module import or a child-process run — never a copied fragment; a sandbox-only pin is a note-level finding at R3.
- **Five reviewer brief templates on disk** next to their skills (`brief-r1.md`, `brief-r2.md`, `brief-r3-standards.md`, `brief-r3-spec.md`, `brief-adr.md`), each carrying the common skeleton (change identity · reviewer session requirements incl. family separation and two-axes-two-sessions · inputs as pinned-state paths · scope boundary · findings grammar · output filename per `reviewPoint().findings` · advisory-only) and its per-type MUST-DELIVER charter; each SKILL.md gets a one-line pointer.
- **Sponsored cleanup**: the dead `const law` binding and its feeding `lawPath` line at tests/acceptance/review-wiring.test.js:20-21 are removed (review wiring — topically this change's; the mechanical-change fence forbids touching this file outside a sponsoring change).
- **ADR-0010 is amended** (family separation: silence → law with stop-and-ask; two-axes-two-sessions — ADR-0010's body is silent on both (a runner-ergonomics reconfirm note records the two-session practice as precedent, not decision content), and the 20261021-reviewer-not-author change deferred the family question as a Non-Goal): amendment is decision content, so `cdd-adr-review` runs on it at R3. ADR-0008 and ADR-0009 get Reconfirmed-by paragraphs only (this change edits files under their code globs).
- **ADR-0011 is drafted** (added after the R3 ADR-axis review found rules 11a and 7 merited their own ADR): `cdd/decisions/0011-ticks-are-claims-and-pins-hit-real-doors.md` records both law decisions with alternatives weighed; drafted `status: draft` for the human sponsor to resolve at Close.
- New acceptance pin file `tests/acceptance/loop-law-hardening.test.js` (RED first) and smoke pin S36 (brief templates' structural facts).

Follows ADR-0008 (reviews stay advisory findings; the new law changes who may review and how ticks age, not what reviews produce), ADR-0009 (rules 2a/2b extend through the single law module; the store stays the byte mirror), ADR-0010 (amended — see above).

## Solutions Considered

- gherkin (law placement): module-rendered rule 2b extension via a new `REVIEWER_INDEPENDENCE` fact — adopted for clauses 1–2 (ADR-0009 mechanism unchanged, first extension since ADR-0010 shipped); literal `RULES_MD` text — adopted for clauses 3–4 (rules 3–12 are literal in `lib/templates.mjs`; 11a and the rule 7 sentence land there with the byte re-mirror); skills-only guidance — rejected: the incident classes (silent same-family review, stale ticks, fragment pins) are exactly what guidance already failed to prevent.
- gherkin (briefs): static markdown files next to the skills — adopted: consumers are cold reviewer sessions, not code; the law module stays the single law home and briefs cite rule ids without restating law text; drift risk is bought down by smoke S36 + acceptance pins. Module-rendered brief templates — rejected: would make `lib/loop-law.mjs` a second home for procedural prose and multiply mirrored surfaces for no machine-checked gain. Improvised per-spawn briefs (status quo) — rejected: the instantiation floor (pinned read state, family requirement, grammar) is exactly what varies when improvised.
- gherkin (two-session findings): one shared findings file with axis sections — adopted: keeps `REVIEW_POINTS` facts, tasks-template wiring, and RW/SSL pins intact while the sessions split. Two findings files — rejected: forks the pinned R3 path and the tasks-template wiring for no reader benefit.
- gherkin (ADR instrument for clause 1): amend ADR-0010 in place with a dated amendment — adopted: the core decision (independence as rule 2b law) stands; the previously unrecorded family and session-splitting weightings become law. Supersede with a new ADR — rejected: nothing in the core is reversed; a supersede would orphan the 0008/0009 lineage citations. Reconfirm-only — rejected: ADR-0010's body is silent on both — the "a same-family cold reviewer is lawfully cold" wording lives only in the archived reviewer-not-author proposal's Non-Goals, a deferral rather than a recorded position; law tightening while the ADR stays silent would be the silent contradiction the ADR review exists to catch.
- reuse-first search record: `cdd/specs/` (gherkin capability owns loop law — delta lands there), `cdd/archive/` (20261021-reviewer-not-author is the direct precedent and the ADR-0010 source; 20261021-runner-ergonomics supplies the pin-realism incident; 20261021-pin-law-rename sets the store-regen-travels-in-archive-commit precedent), platform (no template-rendering dependency needed — static files).

## Non-Goals

- No validator or guard enforcement of family separation: a findings header is author-written; provenance checking is forgeable ceremony. The law's stop-and-ask duty plus the header recording keep degradation visible (door-1 judgment).
- No change to `REVIEW_POINTS` facts, findings paths, triggers, skills, or modes; R3 keeps one findings file.
- No compact-surface changes (ambient/help/instructions stay as-is): family separation and two-sessions are law content, like the rest of rule 2b beyond the compact phrases.
- No tasks-scaffold re-tick line: the law states the duty; the scaffold already carries tick discipline.
- No vendor names in law text: family wording is relative; concrete families appear only in briefs, findings headers, and records.
- No change to the mechanical-change fence itself: the review-wiring cleanup goes through honest sponsorship inside this change, not a fence exception.
- No separate ADR for the briefs' static-file placement (the T2 bet): the briefs are procedural, cite rule ids without restating law, and are structurally pinned by smoke S36 and LLH6/LLH7 — a conscious call, not silence; a later change wanting module-rendered briefs supersedes this reasoning.
